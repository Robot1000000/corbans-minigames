COPS AND ROBBERS

Hide from the cops. Capture robbers. Rescue friends!

About the Game

Play as the robbers to hide from the cops and free your friends!
Seek out the robbers as a cop before the timer runs out!

Game Information

Minimum Players: 2

Points: 1 for the winning team

Credits

Idea: Hugo
Code: Corban
Head of Map Design: Corban

Rules

Each Cop Gets:

  • A Sword

Each Robber Gets:

  • A Knockback sword
  • Fancy Robber Clothes

Upon being caught by a cop, the caught robber is automaticly sent to the cage.
Walking near the cage rescues the robbers inside.
The round ends when all Robbers have been caught or after 5 minutes.

Maps

Warehouse

This map is obviously themed on a warehouse. Many of the supply crates have great hiding spots. There are also plenty of paintings to destroy or hide behind!
