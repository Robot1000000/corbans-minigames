KIT PVP

Arm yourself. Draw your weapons. Become the Champion!

About the Game

Choose your kit and battle it out in a giant arena, using your role's talents and skills to your advantage!

Game Information

Minimum Players: 2

Points: 1 for the winning player

Credits

Idea: Callan
Code: Corban
Head of Map Design: Corban

Rules

When the game starts, you have 30 seconds to choose a role from the below list.
When the round starts, use your role to your advantage and take out as many players as you can.
Be the last player standing to secure your victory.

Roles

Archer

An amazing sniper role. You are armed with arrows, a bow and crossbow. The bow has power whereas the crossbow has punch, making it effective for dealing with melee players.

Art: Corban

Warrior

Simple and easy to use. You get a stronger sword.

Art: Corban

Golem

It's not very fun to get launched by a golem in survival, but now you can do it to your friends! You get strong armor stats and a high knockback sword, but be warned: you may lose a race to a snail!

Art: Corban

Fisherman

This is more of a prank role, as the fishing rod doesn't do much to help. However, you can just have fun yanking other players around.

Art: Callan

Ninja

I... Am speed!
Run and jump around the map with ease using the agile moves of the ninja. Deal high damage using your diamond sword, but be warned, you only have 2 armor points.

Art: Corban

Beserker

A high damage output on your first strike always wins you. As beserker, deal massive damage and get a slight speed boost. Your armor is low, as you want damage, not blocking!

Art: Corban

Maps

Standard Maps

Biomes

This map relives the wonders of your minecraft spawns, from the best to the worst. Spawning in a desert may seem bad until you find a temple! However, the powdered snow always wins with it's unescapable coldness!

Makers: Corban and Callan

Sudden Death Maps

Nether

The pain of adventuring the nether comes back to players in this painful map, with a fortress, soul sand valley, and even a warped and crimson forest. Just make sure that you don't fall into the lava!
